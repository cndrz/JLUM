BOSH SO2 Training

Basic Occupational Safety and Health for Safety Officer 2

Course Description

This course is designed to provide participants with advanced knowledge and skills in occupational safety and health management. It covers various topics essential for safety officers to effectively identify, assess, and control workplace hazards, as well as implement safety programs and regulations.

Methodology

Method Description
Lectures Instructor-led presentations covering key concepts, regulations, and best practices.
Case Studies Analysis of real-world workplace scenarios to apply theoretical knowledge.
Group Discussions Engage in discussions to share insights, experiences, and perspectives on OSH topics.
Activities Exercises to demonstrate safety protocols, use of PPE, and emergency response procedures.

Modality

Online via Zoom and Face-To-Face

Course Objectives

  • To deepen understanding of occupational safety and health principles and practices.
  • To enhance skills in hazard identification, risk assessment, and control measures.
  • To develop competence in conducting safety inspections, investigations, and audits.
  • To equip participants with the knowledge and tools to develop and implement safety programs.
  • To prepare participants for the BOSH SO2 certification examination.
