Occupational First Aid and BLS

DOLE-Accredited training for low-risk establishments (10 to 50 workers).

Course Description

The Occupational First Aid and Basic Life Support (BLS) Training course is designed to equip participants with the essential skills and knowledge required to respond effectively to medical emergencies in the workplace. This intensive 2-day training combines theoretical knowledge with hands-on practice to ensure that participants are confident and competent in providing first aid and performing life-saving procedures.

It is specifically designed for participants of low-risk establishments with 10 to 50 workers, providing them with the tools to establish, maintain, and facilitate effective life support in a workplace environment until full medical treatment is available.

Methodology

Method Description
Lectures & Discussions Interactive sessions focusing on theoretical workplace first aid and BLS concepts.
Practical Exercises Hands-on training to master essential life-saving skills and procedures.
Scenario-based Drills Realistic simulations to practice emergency response within a workplace context.

2 Days | Intensive Training
