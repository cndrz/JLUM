Competency Framework & Performance Management

Aligning employee performance with organizational goals through effective KPI monitoring and coaching.

Course Description

This course provides participants with a practical understanding of competency frameworks and performance management systems. It focuses on identifying competencies, setting Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), monitoring employee performance, and providing effective feedback and coaching.

Participants will learn how to develop competency models, assess training needs, and improve employee productivity and engagement through data-driven performance evaluations.

Methodology

Method Description
KPI Workshops Hands-on exercises for formulating SMART performance indicators.
Coaching Simulations Role-playing sessions focused on providing constructive feedback.
Model Development Exercises in building competency models and matrices.
Practical Application Case studies on performance gaps and improvement planning.

Learning Outcomes

  • Identify different competency levels.
  • Set SMART KPIs for teams.
  • Evaluate employee performance.
  • Provide effective coaching & feedback.
  • Identify performance gaps.
  • Support succession planning.

Target Competencies

Performance Management | KPI Development | Coaching | Planning & Organizing | Leadership
