Our Story

JL Uni Multiserv Inc. (JLUM Inc.) was founded in 2017 with a bold vision—to bridge the gap between technical expertise, operational excellence, and evolving industry demands. What began as a service-driven initiative has grown into a multi-industry enterprise, delivering integrated solutions across consultancy, training, engineering, and digital transformation.

From its early engagements with local organizations to becoming a trusted partner of government agencies, private corporations, and academic institutions, JLUM has consistently demonstrated its ability to adapt, innovate, and deliver results.

At the heart of our growth is a deep understanding that organizations today require more than just services—they need holistic, scalable, and sustainable solutions. Through continuous improvement, strategic partnerships, and the integration of advanced technologies—particularly through our digital arm, FASTSERV TECH—we have positioned ourselves at the forefront of modern business solutions and process excellence.

Our journey is defined not just by expansion, but also by impact—empowering organizations to operate smarter, safer, and more efficiently.

Anchored in our guiding principle: “We exist to serve and work in collaboration with your needs and goals.” This is not just our tagline—it is the foundation of every partnership we build.

Corporate Values (PASSDPACE Framework)

At JLUM, our culture is guided by PASSDPACE, a values-driven framework that defines how we think, act, and deliver:

  • People First – We value our people and clients as the foundation of our success.
  • Action-Oriented – We respond with urgency, purpose, and accountability.
  • Service with a Smile – We deliver excellence with professionalism and positivity.
  • Sincerity – We uphold integrity, honesty, and transparency in all engagements.
  • Diversification – We continuously evolve to meet the changing needs of industries.
  • Portfolio Excellence – We deliver comprehensive, high-quality solutions across sectors.
  • Achievement – We are driven by results and measurable impact.
  • Customer Partnership – We build long-term relationships based on trust and collaboration.
  • Excellence – We set high standards and continuously strive to exceed expectations.
