Effective Communication Skills

Enhancing workplace interaction, collaboration, and professional relationships.

Course Description

This course enhances participants’ communication skills for effective workplace interaction, collaboration, and professional relationships. It focuses on verbal and non-verbal communication, listening skills, and workplace professionalism.

Participants will learn how to communicate clearly, confidently, and effectively in various business and organizational settings, handling difficult conversations with professionalism.

Methodology

Method Description
Communication Exercises Practical drills for verbal and written clarity.
Active Listening Drills Workshops focused on non-verbal cues and empathetic listening.
Workshops Interactive group activities on workplace collaboration.
Role-Playing Simulating difficult conversations and feedback sessions.
