BOSH SO1 Training

Basic Occupational Safety and Health for Safety Officer 1

Course Description

This course introduces the fundamentals of occupational safety and health (OSH). Participants will learn about key concepts, regulations, and best practices to promote a safe and healthy work environment. Topics include hazard recognition, risk assessment, safety protocols, emergency procedures, and employee rights.

Methodology

Method Description
Lectures Instructor-led presentations covering key concepts, regulations, and best practices.
Case Studies Analysis of real-world workplace scenarios to apply theoretical knowledge.
Group Discussions Engage in discussions to share insights, experiences, and perspectives on OSH topics.
Activities Exercises to demonstrate safety protocols, use of PPE, and emergency response procedures.

Course Objectives

  • Understand the importance of occupational safety and health in the workplace.
  • Identify common workplace hazards and assess associated risks.
  • Apply safety protocols to prevent accidents and injuries.
  • Demonstrate knowledge of emergency procedures and response strategies.
  • Recognize employee rights and responsibilities regarding safety and health.
