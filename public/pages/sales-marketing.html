Strategic Sales and Marketing Excellence

Enhancing customer engagement and business growth through strategic planning.

Course Description

This course enhances the participants’ knowledge and skills in sales, marketing, customer relations, and business development. It focuses on improving selling techniques, communication skills, customer engagement, and strategic marketing planning through practical discussions and workshops.

Participants will learn how to create effective sales strategies, understand customer experience (CX), and develop long-term business partnerships through Service Level Agreement (SLA) concepts. The course also covers market analysis, branding, and performance monitoring.

Methodology

Method Description
Interactive Lectures In-depth discussions on modern sales and marketing concepts.
FGD & Workshops Focus Group Discussions and practical workshops for strategy building.
Role-Playing Simulations of sales scenarios and customer negotiations.
Case Studies Analysis of real-world business scenarios and marketing plans.

Learning Outcomes

  • Apply effective selling techniques.
  • Improve customer experience (CX).
  • Develop strategic marketing plans.
  • Use KPIs to monitor sales performance.
  • Apply the 7Ps marketing mix.
  • Build strong customer retention.

Target Competencies

Sales & Negotiation | CRM | Strategic Thinking | Marketing Planning | Business Development
