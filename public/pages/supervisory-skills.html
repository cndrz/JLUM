Basic Supervisory Skills Training

Equipping new and aspiring leaders with the essential tools for effective team management.

Course Description

This course equips supervisors and team leaders with essential supervisory skills to effectively manage people, tasks, and workplace performance. It focuses on communication, delegation, monitoring performance, problem-solving, and workplace discipline.

Participants will learn how to lead teams efficiently while maintaining productivity, teamwork, and high employee engagement through practical supervisory tools.

Methodology

Method Description
Supervisory Case Studies Real-world examples of team management challenges.
Workshops Practical exercises in delegation and task monitoring.
Simulations Handling workplace discipline and employee concerns.
Role-Playing Conducting coaching and performance discussions.
