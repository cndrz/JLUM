Negotiation Skills Training

Mastering the art of persuasion and achieving win-win outcomes in business discussions.

Course Description

This course provides participants with practical negotiation techniques for handling business discussions, resolving conflicts, and achieving win-win outcomes. It focuses on communication strategies, persuasion, relationship-building, and negotiation planning.

Participants will learn how to negotiate confidently and professionally in various workplace and business situations, ensuring mutually beneficial agreements.

Methodology

Method Description
Negotiation Simulations Immersive exercises to practice bargaining and persuasion.
Role-Playing Acting out complex negotiation scenarios.
Planning Workshops Exercises in preparing for high-stakes business meetings.
Practical Exercises Hands-on training in handling objections and conflict.
