Ergonomics Risk Assessment (ERA)

Identifying, evaluating, and controlling ergonomic hazards to improve workplace efficiency and employee well-being.

Overview

The Ergonomics Risk Assessment (ERA) program is designed to help organizations identify hazards that contribute to musculoskeletal disorders (MSDs), fatigue, and reduced productivity. We provide participants with the practical skills necessary to conduct systematic ergonomic assessments of workstations, tools, and work processes, developing effective controls to minimize risks.

Scope of Training / Service

1. Introduction to Ergonomics

Principles of ergonomics, human factors, and impact on OSH.

2. Assessment Tools

Training on RULA, REBA, NIOSH Lifting Equation, and OWAS.

3. Office & Field Ergonomics

Workstation setup, manual handling, and industrial task analysis.

4. Control Measures

Engineering and administrative strategies for workstation redesign.

Methodology

Method Activity Description
Interactive Sessions Lectures, case studies, and incident reviews.
Practical Exercises Posture evaluation, video analysis, and ergonomic scoring.
Field Assessment Workplace inspection and actual ergonomic walkthroughs.
Workshops Group presentations and development of improvement recommendations.

Service Deliverables

Comprehensive ERA Report | Risk Ratings | Photo Documentation | Prioritized Action Matrix | Management Presentation

Course Objectives

  • Understand human factors engineering.
  • Conduct ERA using RULA/REBA tools.
  • Evaluate repetitive motions and force.
  • Recommend engineering control measures.
  • Reduce musculoskeletal injuries & absenteeism.

Duration & Participants

Awareness: 8 Hours

Basic Training: 1–2 Days

Full Assessment: 3–10 Days

Target: Safety Officers, HSE Personnel, Engineers, Supervisors, and Office/Warehouse Staff.
