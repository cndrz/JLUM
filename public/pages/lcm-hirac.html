LCM + HIRAC Training

Loss Control Management and Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment, and Control

Course Description

This intensive 5-day program integrates the principles of Loss Control Management (LCM) with Hazard Identification Risk Assessment and Control (HIRAC) to provide a systematic, proactive approach to preventing workplace incidents.

Participants will learn how losses occur, how to identify hazards, assess risks, and implement effective control measures aligned with international best practices and Philippine regulatory requirements.

Training Methodology

Method Description
Lecture–discussion Concept building through interactive theoretical sessions.
Case studies In-depth analysis of real industrial incidents.
Workshops Hands-on Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (HIRAC) development.
Group exercises Collaborative problem-solving and presentations.
Simulation Workplace risk assessment simulation.
Practical application Site-based scenarios and real-world application.

Course Objectives

  • Explain principles of LCM and accident causation.
  • Identify unsafe acts, conditions, and root causes.
  • Conduct Hazard Identification across activities.
  • Perform Risk Assessment using severity analysis.
  • Apply the Hierarchy of Controls effectively.
  • Develop and implement risk control measures.
  • Maintain HIRAC registers and documentation.
  • Integrate LCM+HIRAC into Safety Management Systems.
