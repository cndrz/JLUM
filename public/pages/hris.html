Human Resource Information System (HRIS)

A comprehensive, web-based platform that consolidates employee data, organizational structure, and HR processes into a single integrated solution.

System Overview

The JLUM HRIS is a digital-first solution designed to transform traditional HR operations. By focusing on three interconnected data domains—The Organization, The Jobs, and The People—it provides a centralized backbone for all your workforce management needs, ready for remote access and the "new normal."

Core Modules

Module Key Functionality
Recruitment Streamlined hiring with automated applicant ranking, online exams, and end-to-end transparency.
Attendance & Timekeeping Online leave management, shift scheduling, and automated DTR computation integrated with payroll.
Payroll Management Library-based salary calculations, deductions, and contributions with multi-format reporting.
Performance Management Aligned goal-setting (Executive to Employee) with real-time proof of accomplishments and flexible appraisals.
Learning & Development Full training lifecycle management, from course library building to post-training ROI evaluations.
Property Management Lifecycle tracking of company assets, equipment issuance history, and property value dashboards.

Platform Excellence

Data Visualizations

Interactive dashboards for demographics, performance trends, and asset distribution.

Cross-Module Integration

Seamless data flow between recruitment, attendance, payroll, and performance records.
