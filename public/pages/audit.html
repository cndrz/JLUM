Safety Program Audit

Advanced methodologies for evaluating and verifying the efficacy of OSH systems.

Course Description

This course equips participants with the knowledge and skills to conduct systematic safety audits, ensuring compliance with regulatory and organizational standards. It moves beyond implementing safety procedures to verifying their efficacy through rigorous evaluation and verification.

Methodology

Method Description
Lecture and discussion In-depth sessions on audit principles, frameworks, and regulatory requirements.
Audit simulation exercises Practical mock-audits to simulate real-world safety evaluation scenarios.
Checklist development workshops Hands-on training in drafting standardized audit checklists and documentation.
Field-based audit practice Practical application of auditing skills in a real-world or simulated field environment.

Course Objectives

  • Understand audit principles and frameworks.
  • Evaluate safety management systems effectively.

  • Develop comprehensive audit reports and corrective actions.
