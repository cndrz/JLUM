Standard First Aid and BLS

DOLE-Accredited training for medium and high-risk establishments.

Course Description

Standard first aid and basic life support training is a four-day (32-hour) training course designed for participants of medium and high-risk establishments with ten (10) to fifty (50) workers and low, medium, and high-risk establishments with fifty-one (51) and above workers.

Participants will be able to respond to specific medical emergencies before medical professionals are able to intervene. Delivered in a traditional in-person classroom setting which combines lecture and hands-on skills sessions. This course also covers administering cardio-pulmonary resuscitation (CPR) manually and using an automated external defibrillator (AED).

Methodology

Method Description
Theoretical Instruction In-depth lectures covering medical theory and emergency protocols.
Practical Hands-on Training Skill sessions involving mannequins, AED units, and first aid supplies.
Classroom Interaction Traditional in-person setting for direct instructor guidance and peer learning.

Duration & Setting

4 Days (32 Hours) | Traditional In-Person Classroom
