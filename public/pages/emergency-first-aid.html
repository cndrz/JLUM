Emergency First Aid

DOLE-Accredited training for small establishments (9 workers and below).

Course Description

Emergency first aid training is a one-day (8-hour) training course designed for participants of low, medium, and high-risk establishments with nine (9) and below workers. Participants are provided with knowledge on day-to-day emergencies that laypersons may encounter until full medical treatment is available.

This course aims to provide participants with the knowledge and skills necessary to effectively respond to common medical emergencies and provide immediate care until professional help arrives.

Methodology

Method Description
Lectures & Discussions Interactive sessions to build theoretical knowledge of emergency response.
Practical Exercises Hands-on skills practice to reinforce learning of first aid techniques.
Scenario-based Drills Simulated realistic situations to apply skills in real-time environments.

Duration

1 Day (8 Hours)

Course Objectives

  • Understand the Basics of First Aid.
  • Perform Effective Assessment and Initial Care.
  • Administer Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR).
  • Respond to Choking Emergencies.
  • Manage Wounds and Bleeding.
  • Treat Fractures and Sprains.
  • Handle Medical Emergencies.
  • Address Environmental Emergencies.
  • Apply Skills in Practical Scenarios.
