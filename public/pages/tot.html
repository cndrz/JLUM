Training of Trainers (TOT)

Empowering safety professionals with effective adult education and presentation skills.

Course Description

Designed to develop competent trainers who can effectively deliver technical and safety training programs in the workplace. This program bridges the gap between technical safety knowledge and effective adult education, ensuring that safety protocols are communicated and retained effectively.

Methodology

Method Description
Lecture-discussion Interactive presentations covering training design and adult learning theory.
Micro-teaching sessions Practical teaching exercises where participants deliver short training segments.
Peer evaluation Constructive feedback from fellow participants to improve delivery skills.
Coaching and feedback Direct guidance from experienced instructors on presentation techniques.
