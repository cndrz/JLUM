Basic Occupational Safety and Health (BOSH) for Safety Officer 1

This training program provides foundational knowledge in occupational safety and health principles required for Safety Officer 1. Participants will learn the basics of workplace hazard identification, accident prevention, and safety regulations to help promote a safer working environment. The course equips employees with the essential skills to support workplace safety initiatives and ensure compliance with occupational safety standards.

Basic Occupational Safety and Health (BOSH) for Safety Officer 1

The BOSH SO1 Training provides participants with a strong foundation in occupational safety and health principles required for individuals designated as Safety Officer 1. The program focuses on building awareness of workplace hazards and promoting safe work practices in different industries.

Participants will learn how to identify potential hazards, understand basic safety regulations, and apply preventive measures to reduce workplace accidents and injuries. The training also emphasizes the importance of employee participation in maintaining a safe working environment.

This course is ideal for employees, supervisors, and personnel assigned safety responsibilities who need fundamental OSH knowledge to support safety programs within their organizations.
